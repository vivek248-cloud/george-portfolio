About Me.

I’m a UI/UX Designer with a background in Computer Applications and User Experience Design. I enjoy understanding user problems, researching solutions, creating wireframes and prototypes, and turning them into intuitive digital experiences.

I have worked on academic and personal projects involving web design, mobile app design, accessibility, information architecture, and interaction design.

Outside of UI/UX, I design original t-shirt artwork — a hobby that grew into a small print-on-demand collection. It keeps my illustration and typography instincts sharp and taught me how real products reach real people.

I’m currently looking for an opportunity where I can grow as a designer while contributing to real-world products and meaningful user experiences.
